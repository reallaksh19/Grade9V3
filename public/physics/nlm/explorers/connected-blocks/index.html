A 50 N push accelerates two blocks (2 kg and 8 kg) to the right. What is the contact force between the blocks?
💡 Think: Which mass is the contact force responsible for accelerating?

⚡ Governing Physical Model Invariants

1. Common Acceleration ($a = F / M_{\text{tot}}$): Since the blocks remain in unbroken contact and cannot interpenetrate, both blocks share the exact same acceleration.

2. Contact Force ($N_{12} = m_2 a < F$): Block 1 requires force $m_1 a$ just to accelerate its own mass. Only the remainder is transmitted through the interface to accelerate Block 2!

3. Newton's Third Law ($\vec{N}_{1\to 2} = -\vec{N}_{2\to 1}$): The contact force on Block 2 is equal in magnitude and opposite in direction to the contact force on Block 1. They act on different bodies and never cancel on a single body.

4. Push Reversal Asymmetry: Pushing from the light side onto the heavy side creates high interface stress ($40\text{ N}$). Reversing the push direction so the heavy side pushes the light side drops interface stress to just $10\text{ N}$ (a 75% reduction!) with zero change in acceleration.

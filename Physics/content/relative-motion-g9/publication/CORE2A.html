          Hints, answers and repair

          Question AUTHOR-VEC-01

          Hint 1

          Name the frame and read the two signed components first.

          Hint 2

          Apply the right-triangle bridge to the component magnitudes for the speed.

          Hint 3

          Direction needs the signed pair, not the magnitude alone.

          Speed 5 m/s; the velocity points into the north-east quadrant.

          5 m/s

          1. Read the signed components from the declared east/north axes: 3 m/s east, 4 m/s north.
          2. Apply the right-triangle bridge: speed = sqrt(3^2+4^2) = sqrt(25) = 5 m/s.
          3. Keep the original signed pair (3,4) for the direction statement; the magnitude alone cannot supply it.

            Check: Both components are positive, so the vector must lie in the north-east quadrant, consistent with the stated direction.

            Question AUTHOR-REL-01

            Hint 1

            Name the frame; subtract B's components from A's, in that order.

            Hint 2

            Keep the signs through the subtraction before taking any magnitude.

            Hint 3

            Apply the right-triangle bridge only once the signed components are found.

            (6,-8) m/s, southeast; magnitude 10 m/s.

            10 m/s

            1. Subtract B's velocity from A's: (6,0) - (0,8) = (6,-8) m/s.
            2. The eastward component is +6 m/s and the northward component is -8 m/s (i.e. 8 m/s south).
            3. Right-triangle bridge: magnitude = sqrt(6^2+8^2) = sqrt(100) = 10 m/s.

              Check: Reversing the observer gives (-6,8) m/s, the same magnitude, opposite direction.

              Question AUTHOR-REL-02

              Hint 1

              Same subtraction order as the worked A/B example, with M and N in place of A and B.

              Hint 2

              5-12-13 is a right-triangle triple; check the arithmetic against it.

              (-5,12) m/s, north-west of the frame's axes; magnitude 13 m/s.

              13 m/s

              1. Subtract N's velocity from M's: (0,13) - (5,1) = (-5,12) m/s.
              2. The eastward component is -5 m/s (i.e. 5 m/s west) and the northward component is +12 m/s.
              3. Right-triangle bridge: magnitude = sqrt(5^2+12^2) = sqrt(169) = 13 m/s.

                Check: Reversing the observer gives N relative to M = (5,-12) m/s, the same magnitude.

                Question AUTHOR-REL-03

                Hint 1

                Reversal negates every signed component; it does not change the magnitude.

                Hint 2

                Add your answer to the original vector as a check -- the sum must be zero.

                (-6,8) m/s; same magnitude (10 m/s), opposite direction to A relative to B.

                10 m/s

                1. Apply v_B/A = -(v_A/B): negate every component of (6,-8), giving (-6,8) m/s.
                2. Magnitude is unchanged because the right-triangle bridge squares both components: sqrt((-6)^2+8^2) = 10 m/s.

                  Check: Adding v_A/B and v_B/A gives exactly (0,0) m/s -- a standing check for any reversal.

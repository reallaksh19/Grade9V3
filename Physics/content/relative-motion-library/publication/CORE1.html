Relative velocity in a plane · CORE1

Relative velocity in a plane

Intrinsic concept difficulty: HARD

Read these before anything that uses them.
Two directions are fixed for the whole bucket: x counts east and y counts north. A number written against x with a minus sign means west, and against y it means south.
A quantity written A/B is A measured from B. The order belongs to the quantity and is not a label attached afterwards.

This bucket covers Position and velocity of one object measured from another in a plane, at a single instant and over one common interval.
Deliberately excluded: Rotating frames. REL-RELATIVE-VELOCITY states that its axes are parallel and non-rotating, and the extra terms a rotating frame needs are not carried here.
Deliberately excluded: Relativistic composition. Both relations state classical speeds as a condition.

vA/B=vA-vB

For constant velocities, relative position changes at the difference of their common-frame velocity vectors; the same finite-interval derivation also gives average relative velocity.

  1. v_A: Velocity of A in the common frame
  2. v_B: Velocity of B in the same frame
  3. v_A/B: Velocity of A relative to B with parallel nonrotating axes

Applies when:

  1. Same observation times and one common interval.
  2. Parallel, non-rotating Cartesian axes.
  3. Classical speeds; relativistic velocity addition does not apply.
  4. A finite-interval average is not automatically an instantaneous velocity when motion varies.

Where the hard work is.
A number alone does not say where something is. (MEDIUM) The idea is reachable by pacing a room, but it is routinely skipped as obvious and then assumed by every rung above it.
Position measured from the other object. (HARD) Observer order and common time are easily hidden by a memorized formula.
From relative position to relative velocity. (HARD) The student must connect two same-time position statements to one common interval rather than memorize a subtraction rule.
Check direction, magnitude and observer reversal. (HARD) Correct arithmetic can conceal an incorrect vector construction or frame interpretation.
When the simple v_A/B = v_A - v_B relation needs frame qualification (research-boundary note). (HARD) Not a Grade 9 assessment badge: this is a RESEARCH-depth boundary note, included only to show where the taught relation stops applying.

Relative velocity in a plane

Intrinsic concept difficulty: HARD

Question AUTHOR-REL-01

In a common east/north frame, A moves at (6,0) m/s and B at (0,8) m/s. Find the velocity of A relative to B. State its direction and magnitude.

    Conditions:

    1. Constant velocities; common time and parallel nonrotating axes.

    Hints, answers and repair

    Question AUTHOR-REL-01

    Hint 1

    Relative to B means measured from B. Which of the two velocities is the one being subtracted?

    Hint 2

    Subtract axis by axis before doing anything with lengths, and keep both signs; the magnitude is something you compute from the answer, not the answer itself.

    (6,-8) m/s, southeast; magnitude 10 m/s.

    10 m/s

    1. Subtract (0,8) from (6,0).
    2. The southward component is 8 m/s and eastward component 6 m/s.
    3. Using a right triangle, magnitude is sqrt(36+64)=10 m/s.

      Check: Over one second the relative displacement changes by (6,-8) m; reversing the observer gives (-6,8) m/s.
